The ESG Challenge for SMEs

From Compliance Burden to Business Opportunity

🌍 Why Sustainability Reporting Matters

  • ESG reporting is becoming essential for businesses, investors, customers, and banks.
  • Regulations like CSRD require large companies to collect ESG data across their supply chain.
  • Banks increasingly use ESG data to support sustainable financing decisions.

⚠️ The SME Reality

  • No dedicated sustainability teams.
  • Data scattered across utility bills, HR systems, spreadsheets, PDFs, and policies.
  • Reporting takes weeks, costs thousands of euros, and often remains incomplete.

How can SMEs generate reliable ESG reports quickly, accurately, and without hiring expensive consultants?
